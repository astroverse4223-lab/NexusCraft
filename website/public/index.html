What you can play

Everything runs on the one server and one login. Walk into a portal at spawn and you are there.

Vote for us

Voting is how people find a small server — the lists rank by it, and one click puts us higher for everyone. It costs you nothing and you get paid in game for it.

Every vote pays 2,500 coins, and voting on consecutive days multiplies it up to seven times. Every fifth vote is also a rare crate key.

How to join

Open Minecraft

Java Edition, version 26.2. Then Multiplayer → Add Server.

Paste the address

Put play.nexuscraft.net in the address box. The name can be anything you like.

Say yes to the pack

We use our own textures. Accepting them is what makes the place look like ours.
